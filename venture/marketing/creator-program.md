# Creator and UGC programme

Written 2026-09-13. Operational templates for the strategy already decided in
`launch-plan.md`.

## The decision this implements

**Seeding, not paid placement.** Product is given, no fee is paid, and there is
no obligation to post.

That is not modesty about the budget. NL padel creator rates are `UNVERIFIED` —
no rate card could be obtained (`../ACCESS-AND-LIMITS.md`) — and paying an
unknown rate for an unknown audience against an unknown conversion rate is three
unknowns stacked on a business that has not yet proved its first. Seeding costs
the landed cost of a bag and buys the same information.

**Revisit paid placement only when:** a seeded creator has demonstrably driven
orders through a tracked link, and the implied cost per acquisition is below the
allowable CPA in `launch-plan.md`.

## Who to approach

Ranked by usefulness, which is not the same as reach.

| Tier | Who | Why they matter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Club coaches and captains** | Trusted, local, talk to the same twenty players every week, and are the cheapest route to the club channel. Rarely called creators, and the most valuable |
| 2 | **Regional players with 2k–20k followers** | Small enough to reply, real enough to be believed. Engagement matters more than reach at this size |
| 3 | **Padel-adjacent NL lifestyle accounts** | Useful for reach once there is proof to amplify. Not first |
| — | **Large national or international padel accounts** | Skip at launch. They expect rates we cannot benchmark and audiences we cannot convert |

**Selection criteria, in order:** do they actually play outdoors in the
Netherlands; does their audience overlap our customer; is engagement real
(comments from named humans, not emoji strings); is their content quality
usable; would we be comfortable if this person described us honestly in public.

That last one matters. Seeding with no posting obligation means the honest
outcome is the one you get.

## The legal frame — this is not optional

**Gifted product is advertising.** Under EU and Dutch rules a creator who
receives free product must disclose that relationship when they post about it,
whether or not we asked them to post and whether or not we paid.

Three rules that bind us:

1. **We must not ask a creator to hide the relationship**, or suggest wording
   that obscures it. "Just don't mention it's gifted" is an instruction to commit
   an unfair commercial practice on our behalf.
2. **We must not script claims we could not make ourselves.** A creator saying
   "this fixed my elbow pain" is a health claim; that it came from their mouth
   is no defence. The banned-claims list in `product-content.md` §7 applies to
   creator content in full.
3. **Creator content is not a customer review.** It may never appear in the
   review widget or feed a rating. The theme fails closed on reviews for exactly
   this reason.

Required disclosure in the brief, not buried: a clear `#advertentie` or
`#gesponsord` label, or the platform's own paid-partnership tool, visible without
tapping "more".

## Outreach template — Dutch

Short, specific, no obligation. Length is the main reason outreach fails.

```
Onderwerp: Padeltas voor Nederlands weer — mag ik er een opsturen?

Hoi [NAAM],

Ik zag je [SPECIFIEK: video/post over X] — [ÉÉN ECHTE ZIN WAAROM].

Wij maken POLDER: padelspullen ontworpen voor buiten spelen in Nederlands
weer. Een tas met een gescheiden nat vak, een geventileerd schoenenvak, en
PFAS-vrije waterafstotende buitenkant. De meeste padelmerken komen uit Spanje
en ontwerpen voor droog weer — wij niet.

Ik stuur je er graag een op. Geen kosten, geen tegenprestatie, geen verplichting
om iets te posten. Als je hem waardeloos vindt hoor ik dat ook graag — dat is
net zo bruikbaar voor ons.

Als je er wél over post: vermeld dan even dat je hem gratis kreeg
(#advertentie of de betaalde-samenwerking-tool). Dat is wettelijk verplicht en
wij willen het ook zo.

Welke maat / hoeveel rackets neem je meestal mee?

Groet,
[NAAM]
POLDER — [STAD]
[KVK-NUMMER]
```

**Why it is written this way:** it names something specific about them in line
one, states the proposition in three concrete nouns, removes the obligation
explicitly, invites negative feedback (which raises reply rates and produces
better product information), and handles disclosure as our standard rather than
their problem.

## Outreach template — English

```
Subject: A padel bag built for wet weather — can I send you one?

Hi [NAME],

I saw your [SPECIFIC: post/video about X] — [ONE GENUINE SENTENCE WHY].

We make POLDER: padel kit designed for playing outdoors in Northern European
weather. A bag with a sealed wet compartment, a ventilated shoe section, and a
PFAS-free water-repellent outer. Most padel brands are Spanish and design for
dry courts. We don't.

I'd like to send you one. No cost, nothing expected, no obligation to post. If
you think it's poor, I'd genuinely like to hear that too — it's just as useful
to us.

If you do post about it, please mark it as gifted (#ad or the platform's paid
partnership tool). It's a legal requirement and we'd want it anyway.

How many rackets do you usually carry?

Best,
[NAME]
POLDER — [CITY]
```

## Seeding process

| Step | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shortlist against the criteria above. Ten at a time, not fifty | Founder |
| 2 | Personalised outreach — the specific line in sentence one is the whole message | Founder |
| 3 | On reply, confirm size/carry preference and address | Founder |
| 4 | Ship with a handwritten note and a **unique tracked link plus discount code** | Founder |
| 5 | Log in the creator sheet: name, handle, date, product, link, code | Founder |
| 6 | **No follow-up chasing a post.** One message at day 21 asking what they thought of the product — not what they posted | Founder |
| 7 | If they post: thank them, and request usage rights **in writing** before reusing anything | Founder |

Step 6 is the one people get wrong. Chasing converts a gift into an unstated
obligation, which is both the thing we promised not to do and the fastest way to
get a resentful mention.

## Usage rights — request in writing, every time

**Never reuse creator content without explicit written permission.** A post
existing publicly is not a licence, and "they tagged us" is not consent. This
matters most in paid media, where using content without rights is both a
copyright exposure and a platform violation.

Ask for exactly what is needed, and no more:

```
Hi [NAME],

Thanks for posting about the bag — we liked [SPECIFIC THING].

Would you be happy for us to reuse that [post/video]? To be precise about what
we're asking:

• Where: our website, our own social accounts, and paid ads on
  [Meta / Instagram / TikTok]
• How long: [12 months] from today
• Where geographically: [Netherlands and Belgium]
• Edits: we may trim for length and add captions or subtitles. We will not
  change what you said or the sense of it
• Credit: we'll credit you as [@handle] unless you'd rather we didn't
• Cost: [none, or the agreed amount]

If that's fine, just reply "yes, agreed" to this email and we'll treat that as
permission. If you'd rather limit any of it — shorter, organic only, no paid —
say so and we'll work to that.

You can withdraw permission at any time by emailing us, and we'll stop using it.

[NAME], POLDER
```

Keep every reply. The email **is** the licence record.

**If a creator declines or withdraws:** stop immediately, pull the asset from
every ad set, and log it. There is no version of this worth arguing about.

## Tracking

Each creator gets a unique link and code, so the physical and social channels do
not become invisible:

```
utm_source=creator&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=nl_seeding_YYYYMM&utm_content=<handle>
```

Coaches and clubs use `utm_source=club` per the standard in
`measurement-plan.md`. Untracked channels get cut for looking unproductive, which
is how the cheapest acquisition route dies.

**What to measure:** link clicks, code redemptions, and orders. Not follower
count, not likes. A coach with 400 followers who sells six bags beats an account
with 40,000 who sells none — and at this scale that outcome is common enough to
plan for.

## What is not decided, and needs real data

- **Creator rates.** `UNVERIFIED`. No NL padel rate card was obtainable. This is
  why the programme is seeding-only.
- **Conversion rate from creator traffic.** No data exists. It is the number that
  decides whether paid placement is ever worth it.
- **How many seeds produce one usable asset.** Assume it is low and seed in
  batches of ten.
- **Whether coaches will engage at all.** The same open question as the club
  channel, and the most dangerous unknown in the business case.
