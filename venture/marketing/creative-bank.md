# POLDER — creative bank

Written 2026-09-12. Companion to `launch-plan.md`. Market: Netherlands. Language:
**Dutch first**, English only for international/secondary use.

**Nothing here has been produced, tested or run.** No ad account exists
(`ACCESS-AND-LIMITS.md`). There are no performance figures in this document
because none have been measured, and no benchmark CTR, CPM, hook rate or
engagement rate is quoted, because none was obtainable. Every KPI below is
defined **relative to the bank's own median** once the bank is live — a
self-referential standard is the only honest one available before launch.

**The governing insight:** the wet-weather proposition is the only claim in this
business that can be *demonstrated on camera in three seconds*. Water on fabric
beads or it soaks. A compartment is sealed or it isn't. Shoes dry or they don't.
Everything else — heritage, design language, subscription convenience — is
assertion. **Demonstration outperforms assertion, so the bank is weighted
heavily towards things you pour water on.**

### Hooks (the first 1–3 seconds)

H1 Demonstration cold-open (no words, water hits fabric) · H2 Problem callout
("Je tas heeft één vak. Dat is het probleem.") · H3 Negative/confession ("Deze
tas is niet waterdicht.") · H4 Side-by-side comparison · H5 Number-forward
("5.000 mm.") · H6 Seasonal/occasion ("Najaarscompetitie. Het regent.") ·
H7 POV (camera is the player) · H8 Question ("Waar laat jij je natte schoenen?")
· H9 Time-lapse reveal (24h later) · H10 Category callout ("Padeltassen zijn
ontworpen in Spanje.")

### Formats

F1 9:16 demo film, 8–15s, no voice, sound design only · F2 9:16 UGC/creator

### Personas

P2 **Weekendrecreant** — 1–2×/week, mid-range racket, cares but isn't a nerd.

## 2. Copy policy — binding on every asset

### Banned outright

| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| **"Waterdicht" / "waterproof" unqualified** | The product is water-*repellent* to a stated hydrostatic head. Unqualified "waterproof" is a misleading claim under UCPD/Omnibus and a GPSR documentation problem. Say the number, say the limit |
| **Any performance claim about the player's game** | "Speel beter", "meer grip = meer controle", "win meer punten". Not substantiable, and `positioning.md` bans it |
| **Any health or injury claim** | "Voorkomt blessures", "beter voor je pols", "minder kans op schimmel". Absolutely prohibited — this is medical-claim territory |
| **"Was" prices that were never charged** | Omnibus Art 6a: the reference price must be the lowest price applied in the preceding 30 days (`FACT`, secondary sources 2026-09-12; verify with counsel) |
| **Countdown timers that reset; "nog 3 op voorraad" not read from live stock; fabricated purchase popups** | Blacklisted practices. `CLAUDE.md` prohibits them in code too |
| **Any review not from a verified purchaser of this store** | Omnibus. Supplier and marketplace reviews are research material, never social proof |
| **"Getest door pro's" / athlete association** without a signed agreement | Fabricated endorsement |
| **Generic environmental claims** — "duurzaam", "eco", "klimaatneutraal" | Unsubstantiated green claims are specifically targeted. "PFAS-vrije DWR" is sayable **only** with the supplier declaration on file, and phrased as what it is: a chemistry fact, not an eco-badge |
| **"Beste padeltas van Nederland"** or any superlative | Unsubstantiable |
| **Fake UGC** — actors framed as customers | Misleading commercial practice |

### Claims permitted only with a document on file

Nothing below appears in any asset until the named document exists.

| Claim | Required document |
|---|---|
| Hydrostatic head (e.g. "5.000 mm") | Mill spec sheet for the exact fabric |
| "PFAS-vrije DWR" | Supplier declaration |
| "Gescheiden nat/droog vak" | It's physical — a photograph is the evidence |
| "Verzonden vanuit Nederland" | 3PL contract |
| "Prijs incl. btw, geen extra kosten bij bezorging" | True by construction for NL |
| Any durability or lifetime statement | Test protocol, plus the guarantee's published exclusions |

### Required disclosures

- **Creator/affiliate content:** disclosure at the **start**, visible without
  expanding the caption, in Dutch — `#advertentie` or `#samenwerking` per the
  Dutch Reclamecode Social Media & Influencer Marketing. Applies to seeded
  product even with no fee: free product is a material connection. Hidden or
  end-of-caption disclosure is non-compliant. (`FACT` directionally; exact RSM
  wording **UNVERIFIED — no page could be opened**, confirm before first post.)
- **Price:** always incl. VAT. Shipping stated or "gratis verzending vanaf €X".
- **Delivery:** a date range, not a duration ("bezorgd 16–18 sep", not "2–3
  dagen"). This is a positioning pillar; treat it as a required field.
- **Withdrawal:** 14-day right referenced on the landing page, not necessarily in
  the ad.
- **Guarantee:** never mentioned in an ad without the exclusions being one click
  away.

### Tone

From `positioning.md`: plain, specific, slightly dry. **Numbers over adjectives.
Exclusions stated alongside promises.**

- Say: "Hydrostatic head 5.000 mm. Houdt Nederlandse regen buiten. Geen dry bag —
  niet onderdompelen."
- Never: "Ontketen jouw spel in alle omstandigheden."

Rules: no exclamation marks. No emoji in headline copy. No superlatives. Every
promise gets its limit in the same breath — the limit is *why the promise is
believed*, not a hedge on it. Dutch first, and written Dutch, not translated
English. Sentences short enough to read at arm's length on a phone.

### A2 — Water repellency (the demonstration angle)

**C01 · De emmer** — A2 × H1 × F1 × P1 × O8 · **Rank 1**
- **0–3s:** Silent. A full bucket of water poured over the bag, one continuous
  shot, no cut.
- **Visual:** Bag on a wet court, grey sky, floodlight. Macro of beading.
- **Script:** No voice. Water sound. At 6s a single card: *"5.000 mm. Geen dry
  bag."* At 9s: *"POLDER. Ontworpen in Nederland."*
- **CTA:** "Bekijk de tas"
- **Destination:** `/products/starterset`
- **Hypothesis:** A single uncut demonstration out-argues any claim; the refusal
  to overclaim ("geen dry bag") raises rather than lowers believability.
- **KPI:** 3s hook rate; outbound CTR vs bank median.

**C02 · Niet waterdicht** — A7 × H3 × F1 × P3 × O8 · **Rank 2**
- **0–3s:** On-screen text over the beading shot: *"Deze tas is niet
  waterdicht."*
- **Visual:** Same pour, then a cut to the spec card.
- **Script:** *"Hij is waterafstotend tot 5.000 mm. Dat houdt Nederlandse regen
  buiten. Zet hem onder water en hij loopt vol. Dat zeggen we liever nu dan na
  je bestelling."*
- **CTA:** "Lees de specs"
- **Destination:** ⚑ `/pages/regen`
- **Hypothesis:** In a category of overclaim, the disclaimer *is* the
  differentiator. Stated limits convert the sceptical gear-buyer.
- **KPI:** CTR; time on `/pages/regen`; ATC rate of that page's traffic.

**C03 · 5.000** — A2 × H5 × F3 × P3 × O8

- **CTA:** "Specs"  **Destination:** ⚑ `/pages/regen`
- **Hypothesis:** A number stops the scroll where an adjective doesn't; cheapest
  asset in the bank to produce.
- **KPI:** CPM and CTR of a static vs the video concepts — tests whether the
  category needs motion at all.

**C04 · Kofferbak** — A2 × H7 × F1 × P1 × O1

**C05 · Regen in beeld** — A2 × H6 × F1 × P2 × O8

### A5 — The grip cycle (the retention engine)

**C15 · Versleten vs nieuw** — A5 × H4 × F5 × P1 × O3 · **high priority, but
retargeting-only**
- **0–3s:** Two grips, macro, side by side. One shiny and compressed, one matte.
- **Visual:** A hand squeezing each; the worn one slips visibly.
- **Script:** *"Links: vier weken. Rechts: nieuw. Je merkt het pas als je het
  vergelijkt."* Then: *"Abonnement. Jij kiest hoe vaak."*
- **CTA:** "Kies je interval" **Destination:** ⚑ `/pages/abonnement`
- **Hypothesis:** Grip wear is invisible until contrasted. The comparison creates

- **KPI:** Subscription attach rate from this traffic. **Not cold CPA** — a grip
  subscription cannot fund cold acquisition at a €39–49 order value
  (`unit-economics.md`), so this runs to warm audiences only.
- **Compliance note:** No claim that a worn grip affects play quality, causes
  slipping injuries or loses points. Show the physical state; stop there.

**C16 · Nooit meer denken aan grips** — A5 × H2 × F3 × P2 × O3

- **KPI:** Attach rate; subscription cancel rate in first 60 days.

### A6 / A8 / A9 — Set, delivery, guarantee

norm)**. This is a judgement ranking, not a prediction — no performance data
exists to rank on, and pretending otherwise would be a fabrication.

**Why not the grip concepts (C15–C17) in the first five:** a €39–49 grip order
cannot fund cold acquisition at all (`unit-economics.md` — the €49 row leaves
almost nothing, the €39 row is not achievable). Grip creative belongs in
retargeting and lifecycle, where it is measured on attach rate, not CPA. Putting
it in cold prospecting would buy customers at a loss on purpose.

**Why not the gift concepts first:** they are seasonal and unusable before
week 11. Shoot them in week 9.

### One shoot day covers ranks 1–4

C01, C02, C06 and C07 share a location, a bag and a hose. C09 needs the same
location plus 24 hours of a fixed camera. **Shoot all of them on the first wet
day after the sample arrives.** In the Netherlands in autumn, waiting for rain is
not a scheduling risk — waiting for a *sample* is.

Production principles, from `positioning.md`: real grey weather, never studio
sunshine; restrained palette, one high-visibility accent reserved for CTAs only;
water visible on fabric in every hero frame; one grotesque, two weights.

**All imagery must be original.** Supplier images carry unclear commercial rights
and, in this category, show the wrong weather.

## 5. Test discipline

- Ship the five ranked concepts into **one ad set**, not five. At the budget in
  `launch-plan.md` §5, five separate ad sets each get too few events to mean
  anything, and the auction ranks creative faster than a split test can.
- **One variable per iteration.** If C01 underperforms, change the hook before
  the angle, and the angle before the format. Changing three at once means the
  next result teaches nothing.
- Do not judge a creative below ~50 link clicks (`ASSUMPTION`, reasoning in
  `launch-plan.md` §5).
- Every winning hook gets three variants before the angle is declared proven —
  otherwise you've found a good film, not a good argument.
- Log every concept's outcome in this file. **This bank is only worth anything if
  it accumulates results.** An untested concept and a failed concept must be
  distinguishable six months from now.
