## The short version

We set what the shop needs to work. **Anything for analytics or advertising is
set only if you agree, and not before.** You can change your mind at any time.

## Why consent comes first here

Under EU ePrivacy rules, implemented in the Netherlands through the
Telecommunicatiewet, cookies and similar identifiers that are not strictly
necessary require consent **before** they are placed. Not after the first page
view, and not on the basis of continued browsing.

Practically, that means:

- No pre-ticked boxes
- Refusing is as easy as accepting — equally prominent, one click
- Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it
- We record what you consented to, and when
