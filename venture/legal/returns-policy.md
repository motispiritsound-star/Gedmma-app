## Your right to change your mind

You may cancel your order within **14 days** of receiving it, for any reason.
You do not have to give one.

The 14 days start the day you — or someone you nominate — physically receives
the goods. If your order arrives in several parcels, the period starts when the
last one arrives.

## How to cancel

Use the cancellation form in your account, or email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. Telling
us before the 14 days are up is enough; the goods can follow afterwards.

> **[LEGAL REVIEW — ITEM 1]** An electronic withdrawal function ("withdrawal
> button") is reported to become mandatory for online distance contracts from
> **19 June 2026**. Sources disagree on whether it applies to goods or only to
> financial services. If it applies, a link in an email is not sufficient — a
> functioning interface element is required. **Confirm scope before launch.**

## Your refund

We refund within **14 days** of receiving the goods back, or of your proof of
postage, whichever comes first. Refunds go to the original payment method, at no
charge.

**We refund the goods and the standard outbound delivery cost.** If you chose a
faster or more expensive delivery option, we refund the standard rate.

## Using the goods before you return them

You may handle the goods as you would in a shop — open the bag, look inside, try
the straps. If you use them beyond that and the value drops as a result, we may
deduct the difference.

To be concrete rather than vague: unpacking and inspecting a bag is fine. Taking
it to a court and playing with it is not.

## If something is wrong with the product

Separate from the 14 days above, and it does not expire with them.

You have a **statutory right to goods that conform to the contract for at least
two years**. If something fails, is faulty, or is not as described, you are
entitled to repair or replacement, and to a price reduction or refund where
those are not possible.

**For the first 12 months, a fault is assumed to have been there from the start**
unless we can show otherwise. You do not have to prove anything.

This is your legal right. It is not something we are giving you, and nothing in
this policy reduces it.
