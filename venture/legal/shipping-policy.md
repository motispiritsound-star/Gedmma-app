## Duties and VAT

**All prices include Dutch VAT. There is nothing to pay on delivery.**

This is worth stating plainly and prominently, because unexpected charges at the
door are one of the most reliable sources of complaints and chargebacks against
cross-border sellers — and avoiding them is a genuine advantage of shipping from
inside the EU.
