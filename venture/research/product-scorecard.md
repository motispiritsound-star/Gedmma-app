## Read the caveat before the ranking

Three dimensions — **grossMarginPotential, contributionMarginPotential, supplierQuality** — are unscored for every
candidate, because no supplier has been contacted and none could be from this
environment. That is **37.5% of the scoring model left blank.**

This is the honest position, and it is worth stating plainly rather than
burying: **the brief asks for a product to be chosen on a weighted score, and a
third of that score cannot be computed without a supplier quote.** Filling those
cells with plausible numbers would produce a ranking that looks decisive and
means nothing.

> Every candidate is missing the same dimensions, so the gaps are common-mode and the RELATIVE ranking is informative. Absolute scores are not, and no pass/fail threshold may be applied until the supplier-dependent dimensions are filled.

> **Separation between first and second: 10.4 points.** The leader is separated by more than 5 points on known dimensions.
